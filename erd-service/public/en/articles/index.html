Read, model, then verify

Each guide includes a concrete change request, SQL or constraint checks, and the limits of the simplified model. Start with the e-commerce lab to reproduce a historical-price error and a multiplied-total error with downloadable data.

The examples are fictional teaching cases. Use your own rules for account deletion, payment retries, tax, and data retention before applying a schema to a real service.
